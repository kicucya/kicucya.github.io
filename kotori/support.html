Quick Help

  • Record by voice: tap the mic and say one line, e.g. “Starbucks 600 yen”.
  • Siri & Shortcuts: add Kotori's shortcut and log an expense without opening the app.
  • Categories: Kotori learns from your corrections — fix a category once and it remembers, all on your device.
  • Apple Intelligence: on supported devices it speeds up learning; the app works without it too.
  • Privacy: your data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
