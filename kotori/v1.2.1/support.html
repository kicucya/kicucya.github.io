Allocate your budget and keep amounts in place

Where to start
More → Budget

Step by step

  1. In Included Wallets, include all wallets or choose the ones to budget for. Tap Total Budget and enter an amount.
  2. Tap a category amount, enter the value with the numeric keyboard, then tap the checkmark. Confirming the amount automatically locks that category.
  3. The lock icon is to the left of the category name. While locked, its slider cannot change the amount, and moving other sliders will not reduce it.
  4. You can still tap a locked amount and edit it with the numeric keyboard. To adjust it with the slider, tap the lock icon to unlock it.
  5. Choose the budget alert percentage, then tap Save. If your allocations exceed the total budget, adjust the amounts before saving.

Frequently asked questions

Does hiding chat history delete my records?

No. In More → Recording → Chat History Range, choose hidden, one hour, one week, one month, one year, or all history. The change applies on the next launch. Your transactions remain available in Transactions, Reports, and exports. Hide Kotori’s Chatter hides greetings and small talk while keeping the prompts and errors needed for recording.

Can I rename a shared wallet?

The owner can rename it in More → Wallets. Participants cannot rename it. If the operation does not complete, follow the guidance shown in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no password.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you enable sync, it syncs across your devices through your own iCloud — never to the developer's servers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging and learning happen entirely on-device.

Can I share with my family?

Yes — share a ledger with family or a partner through your own iCloud. No dedicated servers, no sign-ups.

Can I migrate from another app or CSV?

Yes — import CSV data exported from other apps.

What if a category is wrong?

Fix it once and Kotori remembers — it learns on-device.

Is it free?

Basic features are free to use.

Can I use a previous entry to add a new one?

Touch and hold an entry in Transactions and choose Copy. The amount, content, category, and other details are filled in, with today's date. Change what is different and tap Add. Copy is also available on cards in Record and entries in Reports.

Can I copy just the text I sent?

In Record, touch and hold a message you sent and choose Copy. This copies the original text without adding an entry.

Do typed entries need to use the voice input language?

No. More → Recording → Voice Input Language sets the language used to transcribe speech. It does not restrict the language of text entered with the keyboard.

Where can I change sounds and vibration?

On your iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Kotori. You can change Sound and Vibration separately.

Can I choose which wallet shortcuts record into?

Open More → Wallets → Shortcut wallet and choose a wallet or Use selected wallet. Choosing a fixed wallet does not change the wallet currently open in the app. This setting stays on each device. If the specified wallet is deleted, shortcuts return to using the selected wallet.

Which entries count toward the daily spending alert?

It includes today's expenses that have already occurred across all available wallets, including pending entries. Transfers between wallets do not count. Set an amount in More → Notifications → Daily Spending Alert to receive one alert on each day that reaches it. Watch setup through reviewing the transactions

Do budgets and reports include the same wallets?

Reports, charts, budgets, and their transaction details all use the wallets chosen in More → Budget → Included Wallets. Switching the wallet used for new entries does not change this selection. The selected scope is shown at the top of Reports. All Wallets also includes wallets created later; choosing individual wallets keeps that specific selection. Past spending remains available in transaction details when a category is hidden or deleted.

Can I change a budget amount while it is locked?

Yes. The lock only prevents changes from sliders. Tap the amount to edit it with the numeric keyboard. Confirming with the checkmark locks it automatically. To adjust it with the slider, unlock it using the icon to the left of the category name. Budget setup steps

Where can I set up budget alerts?

Open More → Notifications → Budget Alerts to choose alerts for the total budget and individual categories. Budget Alert in the budget settings starts at 80%. Choose the percentage for an early alert; setting it to 100% turns off only that early alert. It does not turn off alerts for expected or actual overspending.

Expected overspending includes future-dated expenses already recorded in Kotori. It does not predict purchases that haven't been recorded. Tap a budget banner or iPhone notification to open Reports for the relevant period.

Where do spending and budget alerts appear?

In Record, they appear as chat messages. On other screens, they appear as a banner at the top. Tap a banner or iPhone notification to open the daily details or report; chat messages do not open them. To receive notifications while the app is in the background, enable iOS Notifications for that alert and allow notifications in iPhone settings. Timed reminders to record are also under More → Notifications.

How do I change the background color, photo, and color overlay strength?

Open More → Appearance and choose Color or Photo under Background. Both options let you choose a color. For a photo, Color overlay strength controls the color overlaid on it: 0% shows the original photo, while 100% shows only the selected color. Preview changes as you adjust the slider, then save. To reset just the background, choose Default in the Background section and save. Your accent color, appearance mode, and chat avatar stay the same.

Can I use a photo as the chat avatar?

Open More → Appearance and choose a photo under Chat Avatar near the bottom. Adjust its position and size using the circular preview, then save the Appearance settings. You can restore the default avatar in the same section.
