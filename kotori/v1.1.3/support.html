Written guides

Quick Help

You can also open this page from the app: More → User Guide.

  • Record by voice: hold the mic button and say one line, e.g. “Starbucks $4.99”.
  • Siri & Shortcuts: add Kotori's Shortcut to log an expense without opening the app.
  • Categories: Kotori learns from your corrections — fix a category once and it remembers, all on-device.
  • Apple Intelligence: on supported devices it speeds up learning; the app works without it too.
  • Privacy: your data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Shortcuts & Math Input

There are three ways to record without opening Kotori: tap a shortcut in the Shortcuts app, tap an icon on the Home Screen, or ask Siri. These instructions use Record Entry, marked with a microphone icon, to enter the place or item and amount in one line.

Set up once: create a personal shortcut

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on iPhone, then choose Library → Kotori. The button on Kotori's Siri guide also opens the Shortcuts app.
  2. Touch and hold Record Entry with the microphone icon, then choose New Shortcut.
  3. If a description screen appears, tap Editor or Edit. Tap the action's Entry field and choose Ask Each Time. This lets the shortcut ask for a new entry every time it runs.
  4. Use the back button to return to the list and save the shortcut. Find it under Library → All Shortcuts.

1. Tap to run in the Shortcuts app

  1. Open Library → All Shortcuts and find the personal shortcut you created.
  2. Tap the round play button (▶) at the top right of its card. The card in the video is named “Record spending”.
  3. When “What would you like to record?” appears, enter a line such as “Coffee 4.50 USD”, then tap Done.

Watch: tap a shortcut to record an entry

2. Add an icon to the Home Screen

  1. Open the card for your personal shortcut and go to its editor.
  2. Tap the shortcut name at the top, then choose Add to Home Screen from the menu.
  3. Check the display name and icon, then tap the checkmark (Add) at the top right.
  4. Return to the Home Screen and tap the new icon. If it is not visible, swipe left or right to check the other pages. Enter the description and amount, then tap Done.

Watch: add a Home Screen icon and record an entry

3. Give the shortcut a short name for Siri

  1. In your personal shortcut's editor, tap the name at the top and choose Rename.
  2. Enter a short name that is easy to say, such as “Log my spending”, and confirm it. Use the back button to return to the list and check that the card shows the new name.
  3. Activate Siri and say the shortcut's name. When asked for the entry, say the place or item and amount. Try the name on your iPhone to check that Siri calls the intended shortcut.

Watch: create a shortcut and choose a name for Siri. The video shows creation and renaming; it does not demonstrate running the shortcut with Siri or speech recognition.

Siri uses the shortcut's own name. Changing only the icon's display name in Add to Home Screen does not change the phrase used to call it with Siri.

Use Kotori's suggested Siri phrase: you can also call Kotori without creating a personal shortcut. In Kotori, open More → Record Entry / Siri and check the phrase shown there. Activate Siri, say that phrase, then answer with an entry such as “Coffee 4.50 USD” when asked.

On some iOS versions, Kotori appears under App Shortcuts, Use in New Shortcut is in the card's “…” menu, and Add to Home Screen is under the info button (ⓘ) → Details. If Ask Each Time does not appear, touch and hold the input field.

After saving: the entry is stored in Kotori as pending confirmation. Open the Transactions tab, review the amount and category, and confirm it. See Review Pending Entries.

For help with the iOS screens, see Apple's guides to app shortcuts, adding a shortcut to the Home Screen, and renaming a shortcut.

  • Wording your line: name the place (or the item), then the amount. For example “Starbucks $4.99” or “Corner store $6.20”.
  • Math input: put a calculation where the amount goes and Kotori works it out as it logs. “Groceries 12.50*4” is logged as $50.00. The operators are + × ÷, and you can write × as *. This works in one-line entry (quick logging); in the manual edit screen, use the calculator keypad in the amount field. Available in version 1.1.0 and later.

Review Pending Entries

  • Where to find them: open the Transactions tab. Entries that need confirmation are grouped near the top.
  • Review an entry: tap it, check details such as the amount and category, then confirm. To confirm the group together, use Confirm All at the right of its heading.
  • In reports: from version 1.1.2, pending entries count toward report totals, category breakdowns, and budget usage. Confirming an entry does not count it twice.

Keyword Management

  • What it does: browse and edit the keywords Kotori has learned (store names and more) — change which category a keyword is filed under, link different names for the same place into a group, delete learning data, or use Learn from History.
  • Where to find it: the More tab → Keyword Management.

How to Read Reports

  • Where to find it: the Reports tab. Switch between Month and Year at the top and use the arrows to move between periods. Tap the period label to jump straight to any month or year that has records (empty periods are skipped).
  • Summary: expenses, income, and the balance for that period. Pending entries are included in totals, breakdowns, and budget usage. Spending in currencies other than your main one is listed separately under “Other currencies”.
  • Pie chart: it starts with the income/expense split. Tap expenses to see the breakdown by category, then tap a category to see its subcategories. Use the “<” at the top left to go back a level. In a period with no income, the chart opens on the expense categories directly.
  • Budgets: overall budget usage plus budget and actuals per category. Tap a category to see its subcategories, then tap a subcategory row to list that period's transactions right there. Tap a transaction to edit it, swipe left to delete it, and tap the row again to collapse the list. A category without subcategories opens its transaction list straight away. Available in version 1.1.0 and later.
  • Yearly view: switch to Year to see the whole year, with budgets compared at 12× the monthly amount. Whatever you drilled into stays selected when you change the period.
  • Setting budgets: the More tab → Budget.

Set Up Recurring Expenses & Income

  • Where to find it: More → Recurring. Use the add button to set the description, amount, category, wallet, and other details, then save. Tap an existing rule to edit it, or use its switch in the list to turn it on or off.
  • Schedule: choose daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly and set the weekday or date as needed. For monthly and yearly rules that fall on a weekend, keep the date unchanged or move it to the previous Friday or next Monday.
  • End date: turn on Set end date and choose a date. The rule remains active through that day. Opening or returning to the app on this device after that date turns the rule off. Existing entries are kept.
  • Using multiple devices: the end date is saved only on this device and does not sync through iCloud. The on/off state syncs, but other devices may still create entries after the end date. Update each device to version 1.1.2 or later and set the end date separately on each one.

Swipe to Edit or Delete

  • Swipe right: reveal editing actions, such as Edit or Rename. Swipe left for actions such as Delete. Some screens offer Restore or Undo Import instead.
  • When there is one action: swipe most of the way across, or reveal the button with a short swipe and swipe again in the same direction. This runs the action. If a confirmation is required, review it before continuing.
  • When there are several actions: choose a revealed button. Existing tap and long-press controls remain available.
  • Category icons: an icon is optional. Clear just the icon in the category editor and save to show the category without one.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no password.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you enable sync, it syncs across your devices through your own iCloud — never to the developer's servers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging and learning happen entirely on-device.

Can I share with my family?

Yes — share a ledger with family or a partner through your own iCloud. No dedicated servers, no sign-ups.

Can I migrate from another app or CSV?

Yes — import CSV data exported from other apps.

What if a category is wrong?

Fix it once and Kotori remembers — it learns on-device.

Is it free?

Basic features are free to use.
