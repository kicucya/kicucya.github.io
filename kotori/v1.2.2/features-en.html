Everything an expense tracker needs.

The just-talk simplicity stays. Multiple wallets, family sharing, budgets, reports, multi-currency, and import — everything your day-to-day finances need.

🎙️ Input

Log without opening the app

Record from Control Center or your Home Screen without opening Kotori. From Control Center → From the Home Screen →

Choose where shortcut entries go

Pick a wallet for shortcuts and keep recording there, even while viewing a different wallet in the app. Or let shortcuts follow your selected wallet. See the setting

Check pending entries in Control Center

See your pending count at a glance in Control Center. Tap it to open the entries and review them. Watch the guide

One-line logging

Just say “Starbucks, $4.99.” The amount and category are figured out and logged automatically. A store name you say is kept as-is.

Math in one line

Put a calculation in your line and Kotori does the math. “Groceries 12.50*4” is logged as $50.00. Available in version 1.1.0 and later. How it works

Review pending entries

Entries that need confirmation are grouped in the Transactions tab. Review and confirm them there; they already count toward report totals and breakdowns while pending. How to review entries

Copy a similar expense

Copy an earlier entry with its details already filled in. Change what is different and save it as a new entry for today. You can also copy just the text of a message you sent. Watch the guide

Voice or text

When voice isn't an option, type it. Either way, the same one line.

🧠 Smarts

On-device learning

Kotori learns your categorization habits on your device. Fix a category once, and it remembers.

A little faster on supported devices

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence speeds up the learning so even a new entry is sorted quickly. It's just an accelerator — the app works fine without it.

Organize uncategorized entries

Touch and hold an uncategorized entry and choose Categorize with AI. When Apple Intelligence finds a suitable category, you can see the result and the updated report totals.

Keyword Management

See and edit where each keyword Kotori has learned (like a store name) gets categorized. Open Keyword Management from the More tab. How it works

🔒 Privacy

Not on our servers

Your financial data is never sent to the developer's servers.

No account

No sign-up. No email, no password.

Sync via your own iCloud

Syncing across devices uses only your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit).

👛 Manage

Multiple wallets

Keep separate wallets by purpose or account, and rename the wallet you're using right where it is. Owners can also rename shared wallets. Watch the guide

Share with family or a partner

Share a ledger to record and manage together. Sharing also runs through your own iCloud — no dedicated servers, no sign-ups.

Recurring expenses & income

Set up repeating entries such as rent or salary. Choose a start date and check when the first entry will be recorded before saving. You can also set the schedule, end date, and weekend handling. Watch the setup guide

Add recurring entries in chat

Send the description, amount, and repeat day, then confirm when to start. Weekly expenses and income are supported too. Watch the setup guide

Reminders to record

Get a reminder at a time and on days that fit your routine. Set up several reminders, then turn them on on the devices where you want notifications. Watch the setup guide

Transfers

Log money moved between wallets as a transfer.

25+ currencies

Multi-currency support, so overseas spending fits right in.

📊 Analyze

Budget across the wallets you choose

Include every wallet or just the ones you choose. Allocate an overall budget and category budgets, then review spending and transactions from those same wallets. Confirm a category amount with the numeric keyboard to lock its slider. You can still edit the amount directly while it is locked. Read the setup guide

Keep an eye on today's spending

Set an amount for the day and get an alert when spending across all wallets reaches it. Tap the banner or iPhone notification to review the day's transactions. Watch the guide

A heads-up as you approach your budget

Get alerts at your chosen percentage, when recorded future expenses would take you over budget, and when you've actually gone over. Choose alerts for your overall budget and individual categories. See the alert settings

Monthly & yearly reports

Review monthly and yearly income and expenses for the wallets selected in your budget. Open and edit transactions within the pie chart or category budget card. Uncategorized and unconfirmed expenses are easier to find, and past spending remains available for hidden or deleted subcategories. How to read reports

Total foreign spending in your primary currency

Enter the exchange rate in either direction or the card’s final charge, and the converted amount updates too. Reuse the latest rate for those currencies and total your travel expenses in your usual currency. Watch the guide

Review each month from payday

Start each budget month on payday to review income and expenses through the day before the next one. Watch the setup guide

Category breakdown

Visualize what you spent, by category.

🎨 Appearance

Make the colors, background, and avatar your own

Make everyday recording feel more personal with a color or photo background. Choose a color to overlay on the photo and preview changes as you adjust the color overlay strength. Choose Light, Dark, or the system appearance. Add a photo as your chat avatar and adjust it using the circular preview. Text adjusts to help it stay readable over your photo or color background. Watch the setup guide

NEW

Choose what appears in chat

Open Recording in More to choose the chat history range or hide Kotori’s chatter. The range applies on the next launch without deleting your financial records. Voice language and shortcut guidance are now together here too. See the settings

Organize categories your way

Add categories and subcategories, then adjust names, visibility, and order. Tap Edit to see the available actions together. Add and edit → Visibility and order →

CSV import

Import CSV data exported from other apps to migrate over.
