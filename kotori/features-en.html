Everything an expense tracker needs.

The just-talk simplicity stays. Multiple wallets, family sharing, budgets, reports, multi-currency, and import — everything your day-to-day finances need.

🎙️ Input

One-line logging

Just say “Starbucks, $4.99.” The amount and category are figured out and logged automatically. A store name you say is kept as-is.

Math in one line NEW

Put a calculation in your line and Kotori does the math. “Groceries 12.50*4” is logged as $50.00. Available in version 1.0.1 and later. How it works

Shortcuts

Ask Siri or run a Shortcut — log an expense without opening the app.

Voice or text

When voice isn't an option, type it. Either way, the same one line.

🧠 Smarts

On-device learning

Kotori learns your categorization habits on your device. Fix a category once, and it remembers.

A little faster on supported devices

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence speeds up the learning so even a new entry is sorted quickly. It's just an accelerator — the app works fine without it.

Keyword Management NEW

See and edit where each keyword Kotori has learned (like a store name) gets categorized. Open Keyword Management from the More tab. How it works

🔒 Privacy

Not on our servers

Your financial data is never sent to the developer's servers.

No account

No sign-up. No email, no password.

Sync via your own iCloud

Syncing across devices uses only your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit).

👛 Manage

Multiple wallets

Keep separate wallets by purpose or account.

Share with family or a partner

Share a ledger to record and manage together. Sharing also runs through your own iCloud — no dedicated servers, no sign-ups.

Transfers

Log money moved between wallets as a transfer.

25+ currencies

Multi-currency support, so overseas spending fits right in.

📊 Analyze

Budgets

Set budgets per category or overall, and avoid overspending.

Monthly & yearly reports NEW

See each month's or year's income and expenses at a glance. Tap through the pie chart and category budgets — income/expenses → category → subcategory — and open that period's transactions right there to edit them. Available in version 1.0.1 and later. How to read reports

Category breakdown

Visualize what you spent, by category.

📥 Import

CSV import

Import CSV data exported from other apps to migrate over.
