Where to start
iPhone Shortcuts app → Library → Kotori

Step by step

  1. In the Shortcuts library, open Kotori.
  2. Touch and hold one of the three preset recording options. This example uses Record Entry.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap the checkmark.
  4. Next time, just tap Record Entry on the Home Screen.
  5. Enter a description and amount, then tap Done. For example, Lunch USD 12.80.
  6. The entry was added without opening the app. Review the pending entry later in Transactions.

iPhone Shortcuts app → Library → Kotori

This recording shows creating a personal shortcut and giving it a short name for Siri. It does not demonstrate running it with Siri or microphone-based speech recognition.

  5. Tap Entry and choose Ask Each Time.
  6. Open the name menu at the top and choose Rename.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no password.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you enable sync, it syncs across your devices through your own iCloud — never to the developer's servers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging and learning happen entirely on-device.

Can I share with my family?

Yes — share a ledger with family or a partner through your own iCloud. No dedicated servers, no sign-ups.

Can I migrate from another app or CSV?

Yes — import CSV data exported from other apps.

What if a category is wrong?

Fix it once and Kotori remembers — it learns on-device.

Is it free?

Basic features are free to use.
